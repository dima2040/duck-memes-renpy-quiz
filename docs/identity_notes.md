# Sprint 3 Identity Notes

Sprint 3 is an iteration pass, not a final art pack. The goal is to make the
quiz-first vertical slice feel more recognizable while keeping all temporary
asset status clear for later approval and replacement.

## Visual Tone

- Absurd school meme comedy with a faux-serious academy mood.
- School spaces should still read clearly first: classroom, corridor, gym,
  library, yard, roof, party/result scene.
- The joke should come from characters treating meme knowledge like sacred
  doctrine inside an ordinary school frame.
- The current palette leans on chalkboard blues, notebook cream, warm gold, and
  small authority/red accents instead of a single flat color family.

## Audio Direction

No third-party audio has been added in Sprint 3. Keep audio release-safe by using
original, commissioned, generated-with-clear-rights, or properly licensed assets.

Useful future hooks:

- short chalk tap or desk knock for quiz question arrival;
- soft confirmation hit for correct answers;
- awkward dry click or classroom murmur for wrong answers;
- low ceremonial sting when МужикБыкКорова delivers a verdict;
- tiny victory fanfare for school legend recognition.

Audio should support timing and feedback without slowing the quiz flow.

## Canon And Approval

Sprint 3 does not add new meme canon. Visual and audio choices can reinforce the
school meme-comedy tone, but any new meme text, catchphrase, named character, or
friend-inspired detail still needs explicit game-designer approval.
